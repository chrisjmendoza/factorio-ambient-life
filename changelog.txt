Version: 0.1.2
Date: 2026-09-07
  Changes:
    - Wildlife now appears around actual trees instead of at random points near the player. Scoring the land said how much life an area deserved but never said where to put it, so creatures drifted over bare ground and concrete.
    - Birds are lighter and now cast a shadow. A dark shape with no depth cue reads as something lying on the ground in a top-down view; the offset shadow is what makes them look airborne.
    - Butterflies and fireflies keep close to the trees they spawn near; birds range more widely.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.1.1
Date: 2026-09-07
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed a crash on load caused by registering a nonexistent on_player_teleported event.
  Changes:
    - Birds and butterflies are drawn roughly twice as large; at the old size a bird was half a tile across.
    - Lightened bird colouring so they are not lost against dark forest.
  Features:
    - Added /al-debug, which reports the tree count, pollution, vitality score and per-kind population targets where you stand.
    - Added /al-here, which spawns creatures beside you to check visibility independently of the spawning rules.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-09-07
  Features:
    - Birds, butterflies and fireflies that appear based on the trees and pollution around each player.
    - Butterflies are the most sensitive to pollution, so they thin out first as a factory grows.
    - Fireflies come out after dark and blink; birds and butterflies keep to daylight.
    - Wildlife is drawn rather than spawned as entities, so it cannot be shot, mined, or collide with a build.
    - Settings for each creature type, a per-player creature budget, an overall density scale, and the pollution level that drives life away.
